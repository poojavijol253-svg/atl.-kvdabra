Testing & improvement

Make the prototype more useful

Define a success measureExample: the alert activates at the target level in 9 out of 10 tests.
Test safelyUse low-voltage battery power. Keep electronics away from water; ask a mentor to supervise.
Collect feedbackAsk a few users to try the model and note what confused them.
IterateChange one feature at a time and compare results.

About this prototype

ATL Innovate is an independent learning prototype, not an official NITI Aayog or Atal Innovation Mission service. It currently uses browser localStorage and a rule-based chatbot. A production deployment needs authentication, role-based permissions, a secure database, backups, server-side validation, and an approved AI API integration.
